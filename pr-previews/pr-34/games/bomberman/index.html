How to play
  • Blast soft blocks, defeat every enemy, then find the exit hidden under a block.
  • Campaign: move with WASD or arrows, drop bombs with Space or E, detonate remote bombs with Q.
  • Battle: P1 uses WASD + E (bomb) + Q (detonate); P2 uses arrows + Enter (bomb) + Right Shift (detonate).
  • Touch: drag anywhere on your side to steer with the thumbstick; tap 💣 to drop a bomb and ⚡ to detonate. In battle each player grips one end of the device.
  • Power-ups (the letter shown on each pickup):
    • B — extra bomb
    • R — bigger blast range
    • S — faster movement
    • RC — remote detonator (bombs wait until you press detonate)
    • K — bomb kick (walk into a bomb to send it sliding)
    • ST — sticky bombs (your bombs cling to any opponent who walks over them and ride along until they blow)
  • Escape pauses, M mutes, F toggles fullscreen. Bombs chain-explode each other — careful.
  • In battle, when time runs out the arena starts closing in.
