How to play
  • Collect every heart to open the treasure chest, then grab the jewel to clear the room and open its doors.
  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD; on touch screens, swipe or play fullscreen with the joystick.
  • Push emerald blocks to hide from Medusa gazes — push them into water to build a bridge.
  • Some hearts grant two magic shots (Space or X). A shot turns Snakey, Rocky or Alma into a pushable egg; a second shot sends the egg flying.
  • Gols and Skulls wake up when the last heart is taken. R restarts the room, M mutes, Esc opens the map.

A fan tribute to Eggerland 2 (HAL Laboratory, 1986, MSX2). Original world map reference by Benoît Delvaux.
