Reading handwriting

What the recogniser actually does, rather than what it is hoped to do. The first two sections measure the classifier against digits it has never seen. The third is how a corpus of real handwriting gets collected — the numbers that matter are the ones in section four, and they are only worth anything once somebody has written into section three.
