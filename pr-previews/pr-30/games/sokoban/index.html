How to play
  • Push every crate onto a goal pad.
  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD; on touch screens, swipe or play fullscreen with the joystick.
  • Crates can only be pushed, never pulled — one at a time.
  • Z or U undoes a move, R restarts the level, M mutes.
  • Solved levels are remembered, along with your best score.

Levels: Microban (155 puzzles) by David W. Skinner.
