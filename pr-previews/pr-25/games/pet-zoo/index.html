How to play
  • A pet tells you when it eats. Drag the clock hands to that time.
  • The long thin hand is the minutes — it jumps five minutes at a time. The short fat hand is the hour.
  • Watch the short hand creep along as you move the long one. At quarter past four it has already left the 4 — that is how a real clock works.
  • Get one right three times and its egg hatches into a pet of your own.
  • After a few minutes the pets get sleepy and the game stops. You can still wander the zoo while they nap.
  • Grown-ups: press and hold the title for progress.
